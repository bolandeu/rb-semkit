
 Usage: semkit read [IN] agree [OPTIONS]

 The words of each phrase put in agreement.

 The phrase «растаможить машина» becomes «растаможить машину».

 The rules go by neighbouring words, with no syntax parser:
 - a verb, then a noun group: the group in the accusative, by animacy as decline does («купить
 рыжий кот» → «купить рыжего кота»);
 - a noun, then a noun group with no preposition: the group in the genitive («растаможка
 автомобиль» → «растаможка автомобиля», «таможенное оформление автомобиль» → «таможенное оформление
 автомобиля»);
 - an adjective, then a noun: the adjective in the gender, number and case of the noun («легковой
 машина» → «легковая машина»);
 - a preposition, then a noun group: из, для, без, до, от, у the genitive («растаможка авто из
 германия» → «растаможка авто из германии»); к the dative; с the instrumental; в, на the
 prepositional for a toponym («в москве»), and a word in the accusative or the prepositional
 already stays («в кредит»);
 - an indeclinable word («авто», «такси») stays.

 A phrase whose words agree already stays («купить диван»). A phrase stays as it is when a word has
 no rule («быстро», «машина купить», «по» and other prepositions), an adjective has no noun after
 it, a noun is in another case («купить машиной»), or в, на stand before a noun that is no toponym
 («в аптека»).

 The result is a table, a row per phrase: agreed, the phrase with its words in agreement, and
 agree_note, the reason a phrase stays as it is (empty when it agrees); the two columns go right
 after the column of the phrases. A core from word variants: linear-combinations, then agree, then
 a step by meaning (`semkit read variants.csv linear-combinations agree nearest --against
 page_texts.csv`).

 Example: semkit read --text "купить рыжий кот" --text "машина купить" agree write -
 -> купить рыжего кота, ; машина купить, no rule for «машина купить»

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.
 The column agree_note goes right after it.

│ --as          NAME  Write the result to a new column NAME and keep the working column as it is.  │
│ --help              Show this message and exit.                                                  │
