
 Usage: semkit read {IN} extract-complements [OPTIONS]

 Prepositional complements of a phrase, joined by ", "; extract only: «диван для детей с доставкой»
 → «для детей, с доставкой».

 A complement is a preposition of the bundled list complement_prepositions («для», «с», «в» and
 others; any case, ё as е) and the words after it up to its first noun and the oblique nouns and
 adjectives right after that noun («стол из массива дуба белый» → «из массива дуба»), before the
 next preposition. A preposition with no noun after it gives nothing.

 extract-complements keeps only what the matcher finds in the working column; an empty cell where
 it finds nothing.

 Example: semkit read core.csv extract-complements write out.csv
