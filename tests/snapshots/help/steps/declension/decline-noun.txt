
 Usage: semkit read {IN} decline-noun [OPTIONS] [CASE]

 Each word of a phrase, a noun in the nominative singular, declined: «диван» genitive → «дивана».

 case: "nominative", "genitive", "dative", "accusative", "instrumental" or "prepositional".
 number: "singular" or "plural" (12 forms in all; the nominative singular is the word itself).
 Every word is taken as a noun; for a phrase of mixed words use `decline`. A word with a capital
 keeps it («Москва» → «Москвы»); an indeclinable word («кафе»), an abbreviation («МТС») and a word
 the tables lack stay. The accusative comes from the tables only, by the word or its last three
 letters: «парк» → «парк», but «диван» → «дивана» (as «Иван»).

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv decline-noun купить write out.csv
