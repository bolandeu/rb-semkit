
 Usage: semkit read {IN} irrelevant-words [OPTIONS] [WORD]

 The candidates for minus-words around a main word: «чехол» in «чехол для телефона» for «телефон».

 word: the main word, a noun in the nominative singular. A candidate is a word right before a
 preposition (or a pronoun) and a form of the main word in the case the preposition takes («для
 телефона», «на телефон»), when it is no stopword. Toponyms with their prepositions and buy markers
 are cut first («купить чехол для телефона в москве» → «чехол для телефона»). A table with a row
 per candidate in the order of first use: `word` (lower case), `uses` (every phrase counts, repeats
 too) and `examples` (each distinct «word preposition form», joined by ", "). The input is not
 changed.

 The step reads the working column; the table replaces the current one, its column word is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv irrelevant-words купить write out.csv
