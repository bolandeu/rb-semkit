
 Usage: semkit read {IN} decline [OPTIONS] [CASE]

 Every word of a phrase that declines, to the case: «купить красный диван» genitive → «купить
 красного дивана».

 case: "nominative", "genitive", "dative", "accusative", "instrumental" or "prepositional".
 number: "singular" or "plural". An adjective and a noun in its lemma decline; a verb, an adverb, a
 preposition, a conjunction, a particle, an indeclinable word and a word already in another case
 («на дачу») stay; a pronoun declines only as an adjective («этот» → «этого», «он» stays). A word
 right after a preposition or a possessive pronoun that is not the first word stays («чехол для
 телефон» → «чехла для телефон»; «мой синий стол» → «моего синего стола»). A word in «-ой» before a
 word in another case stays too («с большой скидкой»). A word keeps its capitals.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv decline genitive write out.csv
