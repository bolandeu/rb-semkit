
 Usage: semkit read [IN] decline [OPTIONS] [CASE]

 Every word of a phrase that declines, to the case: «купить красный диван» genitive → «купить
 красного дивана».

 case: "nominative", "genitive", "dative", "accusative", "instrumental" or "prepositional".
 number: "singular" or "plural"; a phrase in the nominative plural declines in the plural anyway
 («угловые диваны» → «угловых диванов»).

 An adjective and a noun in its lemma decline; a verb, an adverb, a preposition, a conjunction, a
 particle, an indeclinable word and a word already in another case («на дачу») stay; a pronoun
 declines only as an adjective («этот» → «этого», «он» stays). A feminine, neuter or plural
 adjective declines from its lemma («большая скидка» → «большой скидки»); a hyphenated noun
 declines whole («диван-кровать» → «дивана-кровати»). The accusative agrees by animacy: an
 inanimate noun and its adjective keep the nominative («купить кожаный диван»), an animate one
 takes the genitive («купить рыжего кота»). A word right after a preposition or a possessive
 pronoun that is not the first word stays («чехол для телефон» → «чехла для телефон»; «мой синий
 стол» → «моего синего стола»). A word in «-ой» before a word in another case stays too («с большой
 скидкой»). A word keeps its capitals.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv decline nominative write out.csv
