
 Usage: semkit read {IN} remove-phrase-end [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 The words at the end of a phrase, cut until the last word is clean; remove only.

 kind: "prepositions" (prepositions and the other function words of the stopword list: «диван в и»
 → «диван») or "buy_markers" (the words of the bundled list «sale», whole phrases of it too: «диван
 купить недорого» → «диван»). Words compare in any case, ё as е, as written («для,» with its comma
 stays). A phrase of such words only becomes ''.

 remove-phrase-end removes what the matcher finds from the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv remove-phrase-end prepositions write out.csv
