
 Usage: semkit read {IN} keep-ready-pattern [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 A ready regular expression, found anywhere in a phrase, case-sensitive.

 kind: "domain" (Latin domains with subdomains: «www.example.com»; extract gives all, joined by a
 space), "email" (the first e-mail), "phone" (Russian numbers from +7 or 8: «8 (999) 123-45-67»;
 all, joined by ", "), "date_ddmmyyyy" (the first date as 05.11.2023, 05-11-2023, 05/11/2023 or
 05112023) or "date_yyyymmdd" (as 2023-11-05 … 20231105). A date has the same separator twice, or
 none, and is never part of a longer number; its day and month are not checked (39.19.2999 is
 found).
 find: the pattern is there. remove: every match is cut out. extract: as above, '' where none.

 keep-ready-pattern keeps only the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv keep-ready-pattern domain write out.csv
