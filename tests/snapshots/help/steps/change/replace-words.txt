
 Usage: semkit read {IN} replace-words [OPTIONS] [PAIRS]

 Whole words and phrases of the phrase replaced by `pairs` (what to find → its replacement); case
 and ё do not count. Longer phrases go first: {"стиральная машинка": "стиральная машина",
 "машинка": "машина"}. An empty replacement deletes the words.

 case: "new" — the replacement as given; "keep" — in the case of the first word it replaces (lower,
 capitalised, upper; a word of mixed case keeps the replacement as given).

 pairs: a file of two columns, what to find and its replacement; the first row is the header, the
 file is read like `read` does.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv replace-words купить write out.csv
