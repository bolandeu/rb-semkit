
 Usage: semkit read [IN] translit [OPTIONS] [TO]

 Letters of one alphabet written in the other. The other chars stay.

 to: "en" — «Щука жёлтая» → «Schuka zheltaya», a word in capitals stays in capitals («ЖУК» →
 «ZHUK»); "ru" — by letter groups, «Yandex» → «Яндекс», «Jeep» → «Джееп», «ob'em» → «обэм»: shch is
 щ, ts is ц, yy is ый («krasnyy» → «красный»), y after a vowel and before no vowel is й («Moy» →
 «Мой», «dayte» → «дайте»); a word in capitals is read by the groups of small letters and stays in
 capitals («ZHUK» → «ЖУК»). ь and ъ are dropped in "en" and е and ё both give e, so "ru" cannot
 bring them back («den» → «ден»).

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv translit en write out.csv
