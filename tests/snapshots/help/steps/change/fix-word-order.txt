
 Usage: semkit read {IN} fix-word-order [OPTIONS]

 The words of the phrase in the order of a search query: «диван для гостиной раскладной кожаный» →
 «кожаный раскладной диван для гостиной».

 1. The complements go to the end: a preposition (для, с, без, в, из, на…) with the words after it
 up to the last noun among the next 5, stopping at the next preposition or at `main_word`.
 2. The adjectives that agree with the main word in `form` go first: masculine («кожаный»),
 feminine («кожаная»), feminine_accusative («кожаную»), plural («кожаные»).
 3. The words of the bundled list of words that start a query (где, купить, недорогой, кожаный…, in
 that form) go first, in the order of the list.

 main_word: the object of the query, one word (case and ё do not count): a complement never takes
 it. complements=False: only step 3. The words are joined by one space.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv fix-word-order write out.csv
