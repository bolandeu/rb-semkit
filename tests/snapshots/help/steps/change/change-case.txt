
 Usage: semkit read {IN} change-case [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 The phrase in another case, by `kind`:

 - "lower", "upper": the whole phrase; - "title": every word capitalised, the rest lower; a letter
 after a digit or a sign starts a word too («иван-петров 5g» → «Иван-Петров 5G»); - "sentence",
 "sentence_lower": the first letter of the text and of each sentence (after «.», «!», «?» and
 spaces) upper or lower; the other letters stay; - "latin_title": a word that starts with a small
 latin letter gets it capital («iphone» → «Iphone»); - "latin_upper": a word with a latin letter in
 upper case whole («wi-fi-роутер» → «WI-FI-РОУТЕР»); - "list_title", "list_upper": the words of
 `words` capitalised or upper (case and ё do not count); - "toponyms": the place names of the
 bundled geo list as the list spells them («нижний новгород» → «Нижний Новгород»), the longest name
 from each word; - "abbreviations": a word of 2 to 5 consonants, or 3 to 5 vowels, upper («hp»,
 «ооо»), then each word of the bundled abbreviations as the list spells it («спб» → «СПб», «usb» →
 «USB», «СМ» → «см»).

 The word kinds split the phrase on any whitespace and join the words by one space.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv change-case купить write out.csv
