
 Usage: semkit read [IN] guillemets [OPTIONS]

 Straight double quotes as «ёлочки», opening and closing in turn: «диван "Честер" купить» → «диван
 «Честер» купить». A phrase with quotes loses the spaces at its edges.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv guillemets write out.csv
