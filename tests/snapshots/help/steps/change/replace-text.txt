
 Usage: semkit read {IN} replace-text [OPTIONS] [PAIRS]

 The phrase changed by pairs, one after the other (a later pair works on the result of the earlier
 ones).

 by: what a pair finds:
 - "substring": every occurrence of the text, in any case and ё as е, becomes the replacement as
 given;
 - "phrase": a phrase equal to the text (case, ё and extra spaces do not count) becomes the
 replacement in the case of the phrase: lower, capitalised or upper («МОСКВА» → «САНКТ-ПЕТЕРБУРГ»);
 - "regex": each match of the pattern (cyrillic letters are word chars) becomes the replacement,
 with `\1` and `\g<name>` for the groups.
 first_only: with "regex", only the first match.

 pairs: a file of two columns, what to find and its replacement; the first row is the header, the
 file is read like `read` does.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv replace-text --pairs pairs.csv write out.csv
