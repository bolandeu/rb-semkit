
 Usage: semkit read [IN] match-type [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 The phrase with the operators of a match type.

 kind: the match type:
 - "broad": «A в Б», every «+», «!», «[», «]» and «"» taken out;
 - "plus_every_word": «+A +в +Б»; "broad_modifier": «+A в +Б», the stopwords plain;
 - "stopword_modifier": «A +в Б», the modifier ("+" or "!") on the stopwords only;
 - "fixed_forms": «!A !в !Б»; "quoted_fixed_forms": «"!A !в !Б"»; "quoted_fixed_order": «"[!A !в
 !Б]"»;
 - "brackets": «[A в Б]»; "quotes": «"A в Б"»;
 - the exact match is "quotes", "quoted_fixed_forms" and "quoted_fixed_order" for Yandex,
 "brackets" for Google; the phrase match is "brackets" for Yandex, "quotes" for Google;
 - "antisemantic": «[A] в [Б]», each word but the stopwords in brackets.
 modifier: the operator of "stopword_modifier": "+" or "!".

 A word that has an operator keeps it, and no other is added; the minus-words keep their place, and
 the kinds that wrap the phrase put them after it («"купить диван" -бесплатно»). A phrase already
 in quotes or brackets is not wrapped again: the operators of a word go inside the pair («"+купить
 +диван"»); "brackets", "quotes" and the quoted kinds put their own wrap in place of the old one
 («"купить диван"» → «[купить диван]»), and "antisemantic" drops the old wrap. Stopwords are found
 in any case. An empty phrase stays empty; the words are joined by one space ("broad" keeps the
 spaces as they are).

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv match-type quotes write out.csv
