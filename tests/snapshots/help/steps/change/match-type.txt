
 Usage: semkit read {IN} match-type [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 The phrase with the operators of a match type, by `kind`:

 - "broad": «A в Б», every «+», «!», «[», «]» and «"» taken out; - "plus_every_word": «+A +в +Б»;
 "broad_modifier": «+A в +Б», the stopwords plain; - "stopword_modifier": «A +в Б», `modifier` ("+"
 or "!") on the stopwords only; - "fixed_forms": «!A !в !Б»; "quoted_fixed_forms": «"!A !в !Б"»;
 "quoted_fixed_order": «"[!A !в !Б]"»; - "brackets": «[A в Б]»; "quotes": «"A в Б"»; - the exact
 match is "quotes", "quoted_fixed_forms" and "quoted_fixed_order" for Yandex, "brackets" for
 Google; the phrase match is "brackets" for Yandex, "quotes" for Google; - "antisemantic": «[A] в
 [Б]», each word but the stopwords in brackets.

 A word that has an operator keeps it, and no other is added; the minus-words keep their place, and
 the kinds that wrap the phrase put them after it («"купить диван" -бесплатно»). Stopwords are
 found in any case. An empty phrase stays empty; the words are joined by one space ("broad" keeps
 the spaces as they are).

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv match-type купить write out.csv
