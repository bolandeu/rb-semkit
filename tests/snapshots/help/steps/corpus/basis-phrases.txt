
 Usage: semkit read {IN} basis-phrases [OPTIONS]

 Each phrase that is a basis of the core, or "" for the others: a basis holds no smaller phrase of
 the core.

 A phrase is a basis when it is the first with its set of words and no other phrase of the core has
 some of its words only: «купить диван», «диван», «диван купить» → «», «диван», «». Words are
 compared by case and ё.
 lemmas: compare the lemmas of the words («диваны» is «диван»), by `lemmatizer`.
 stopwords: False leaves the stopwords out of the comparison; a phrase of stopwords only keeps
 them.

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_basis.

 Example: semkit read core.csv basis-phrases write out.csv

│ --stopwords    --no-stopwords          False leaves the stopwords out of the comparison; a       │
│                                        phrase of stopwords only keeps them.                      │

│ --as                             NAME  Write the result to a new column NAME and keep the        │
