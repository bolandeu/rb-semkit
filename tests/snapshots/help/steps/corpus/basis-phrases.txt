
 Usage: semkit read {IN} basis-phrases [OPTIONS]

 Each phrase that is a basis of the core, or an empty cell for the others: a basis holds no smaller
 phrase of the core.

 A phrase is a basis when it is the first with its set of words and no other phrase of the core has
 some of its words only: «купить диван», «диван», «диван купить» → «», «диван», «». Words are
 compared in any case and with ё as е.
 lemmas: compare the lemmas of the words («диваны» is «диван»).
 stopwords: on, the stopwords count; off (--no-stopwords), they are left out of the comparison, and
 a phrase of stopwords only keeps them.

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_basis.

 Example: semkit read core.csv basis-phrases write out.csv
