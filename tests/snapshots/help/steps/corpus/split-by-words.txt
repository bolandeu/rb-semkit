
 Usage: semkit read {IN} split-by-words [OPTIONS] [KEEP]

 The core split into good, bad and rest phrases, and the frequency dictionary of the rest.

 bad: a phrase with any of these words is bad.
 good: a phrase that is not bad and has only these words is good. Every other phrase, an empty one
 too, is the rest. Words are compared by case and ё.
 lemmas: compare the lemmas of the words and of `good` and `bad` («диваны» is «диван»), by
 `lemmatizer`. The parts are the rows of the input in its order: a Series for a Series, the table
 for a table. `frequency` is the `ngrams` table of the rest, of lemmas with `lemmas`, to choose the
 next good and bad words.

 keep: the part of the core to keep: the good phrases, the bad ones or the rest.

 The step keeps only the rows of the part named by --keep.

 Example: semkit read core.csv split-by-words good write out.csv
