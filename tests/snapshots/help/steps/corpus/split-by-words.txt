
 Usage: semkit read {IN} split-by-words [OPTIONS] [KEEP]

 The core split into good, bad and rest phrases, and the frequency dictionary of the rest.

 bad: a phrase with any of these words is bad.
 good: a phrase that is not bad and has only these words is good.
 lemmas: compare the lemmas of the words of the phrase and of the lists («диваны» is «диван»).

 Every other phrase, an empty one too, is the rest. Words are compared in any case and with ё as е.
 The parts keep the rows of the input in its order. The frequency dictionary is the ngrams table of
 the rest (of lemmas with lemmas), to choose the next good and bad words.

 keep: the part of the core to keep: the good phrases, the bad ones or the rest.

 The step keeps only the rows of the part named by --keep.

 Example: semkit read core.csv split-by-words good --good 'купить,диван' write out.csv

│   KEEP      <good|bad|rest>  the part of the core to keep: the good phrases, the bad ones or the │
│                              rest. The same as --keep KEEP.                                      │

│ --good          <str>  a phrase that is not bad and has only these words is good. Items: a comma │

│                        @NAME of a list.                                                          │

│                        (one item per line, or the first column of a table) or @NAME of a list.   │

│                        is «диван»).                                                              │
│ --keep          KEEP   the part of the core to keep: the good phrases, the bad ones or the rest. │
