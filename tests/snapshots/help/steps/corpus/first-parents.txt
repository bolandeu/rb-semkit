
 Usage: semkit read {IN} first-parents [OPTIONS] [PARENTS]

 The first item of `parents` that is a parent of the phrase, or "" when none is. Empty items are
 skipped.

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_parent.

 Example: semkit read core.csv first-parents купить write out.csv
