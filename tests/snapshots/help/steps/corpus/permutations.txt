
 Usage: semkit read {IN} permutations [OPTIONS]

 Every order of the words of each phrase, the phrases one after another: «a b c» → «a b c», «a c
 b», «b a c»…

 A phrase of n words gives n! phrases, a repeated word too; an empty phrase gives one empty phrase.
 limit: the most phrases the result may hold; a larger result is refused before any is made.

 The step makes a table of its result; it replaces the current table, its first text column is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv permutations --limit 1000 write out.csv
