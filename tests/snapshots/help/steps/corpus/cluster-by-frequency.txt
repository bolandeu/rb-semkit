
 Usage: semkit read {IN} cluster-by-frequency [OPTIONS] [THRESHOLD]

 The cluster of each phrase: its words that stand in at least `threshold` phrases of the core.

 A word counts once per phrase; words are compared by case and ё; stopwords are left out. The
 cluster lists its words in lower case, the most frequent first, then alphabetically, so «купить
 диван» and «диван купить» make one cluster. A phrase with no such word gets "".

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_cluster.

 Example: semkit read core.csv cluster-by-frequency 2 write out.csv
