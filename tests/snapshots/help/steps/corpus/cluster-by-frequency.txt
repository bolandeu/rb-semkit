
 Usage: semkit read [IN] cluster-by-frequency [OPTIONS] [THRESHOLD]

 The cluster of each phrase: its words that stand in at least a number of phrases of the core.

 threshold: the fewest phrases a word must stand in to name a cluster.

 A word counts once per phrase; words are compared in any case and with ё as е; stopwords are left
 out. The cluster lists its words in lower case, the most frequent first, then alphabetically, so
 «купить диван» and «диван купить» make one cluster. A phrase with no such word gets an empty cell.

 Example: semkit read --text "купить диван" --text "диван купить" --text "купить кресло"
 cluster-by-frequency 2 write -
 -> купить диван, купить диван; диван купить, купить диван; купить кресло, купить

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_cluster.

╭─ Arguments ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│   THRESHOLD      <int>  the fewest phrases a word must stand in to name a cluster. The same as   │
│                         --threshold THRESHOLD.                                                   │

╭─ Options ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
