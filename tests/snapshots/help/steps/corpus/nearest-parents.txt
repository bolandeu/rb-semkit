
 Usage: semkit read {IN} nearest-parents [OPTIONS] [PARENTS]

 The parent from `parents` with the most words for each phrase, or "" when it has none.

 Between parents with as many words, the one whose words stand later in the phrase wins («a b c»:
 «b c» before «a c»), then the first in the list. Empty items of `parents` are skipped.

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_parent.

 Example: semkit read core.csv nearest-parents купить write out.csv
