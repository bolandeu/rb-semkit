
 Usage: semkit read {IN} first-children [OPTIONS] [CHILDREN]

 The first item of `children` that is a child of the phrase, or "" when none is; an empty phrase
 has none.

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_child.

 Example: semkit read core.csv first-children купить write out.csv
