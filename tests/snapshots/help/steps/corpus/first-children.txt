
 Usage: semkit read [IN] first-children [OPTIONS] [CHILDREN]

 The first item of a list that is a child of the phrase, or an empty cell when none is; an empty
 phrase has none.

 children: the phrases that may be children, in the order to try; a child is a phrase that holds
 every word of the phrase.

 Example: semkit read --text "диван" --text "кресло" first-children "купить диван в москве,угловой
 диван" write -
 -> диван, купить диван в москве; кресло,

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_child.

│   CHILDREN      <str>  the phrases that may be children, in the order to try; a child is a       │
│                        phrase that holds every word of the phrase. Items: a comma list 'a,b',    │
│                        @FILE (one item per line, or the first column of a table) or @NAME of a   │
│                        list. The same as --children CHILDREN.                                    │
