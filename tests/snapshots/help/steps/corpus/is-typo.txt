
 Usage: semkit read {IN} is-typo [OPTIONS]

 true for a phrase with a misspelt word, false for the others.

 By default the words are learnt from the phrases given, so give the whole core: a word is misspelt
 when another word stands in at least 10 times as many phrases and differs from it by a few edits
 (up to 2, and one per two letters after the first for a short word: «диавн» → «диван»). A word
 with a digit, a stopword and a wordform the bundled dictionary or pymorphy3 knows («москве»,
 «угловые») are never misspelt; a correction starts with the same letter as the word.

 The step writes its result to a new column <column>_typo.

 Example: semkit read core.csv is-typo write out.csv
