
 Usage: semkit read {IN} synonyms [OPTIONS]

 The synonyms in a search-phrase report: a table word, sure, synonyms (joined by a space).

 A row whose condition and matched phrase differ in one word gives a sure synonym; a row with no
 matched phrase («-») compares the condition with the phrase and gives a synonym to check, unless
 it is a sure one already. The word is the one of the condition. Words are compared as lemmas, in
 any case and with ё = е; minus-words, operators (+ ! " [ ]) and stopwords are left out. The sure
 rows come first, each part in the order of the report.

 The step makes a table of its result; it replaces the current table, its first text column is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read report.csv synonyms write out.csv
