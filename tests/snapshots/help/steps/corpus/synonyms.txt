
 Usage: semkit read {IN} synonyms [OPTIONS]

 The synonyms in a search-phrase report: a table word, sure, synonyms (joined by a space).

 report: a table with the columns phrase, condition and matched. A row whose condition and matched
 phrase differ in one word gives a sure synonym; a row with no matched phrase («-») compares the
 condition with the phrase and gives a synonym to check, unless it is a sure one already. The word
 is the one of the condition. Words are compared as lemmas (by `lemmatizer`), in any case and with
 ё = е; minus-words, operators (+ ! " [ ]) and stopwords are left out. The sure rows come first,
 each part in the order of the report.

 The step makes a table of its result; it replaces the current table, its first text column is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv synonyms write out.csv
