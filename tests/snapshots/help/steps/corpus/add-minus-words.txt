
 Usage: semkit read {IN} add-minus-words [OPTIONS] [SYNONYMS]

 Each phrase with the synonyms of its words appended as minus-words: «купить диван» → «купить диван
 -софа».

 table: a table with the columns word and synonyms (separated by spaces), as `synonyms` gives. A
 word of the phrase matches by its lemma (by `lemmatizer`), in any case and with ё = е. A synonym
 that the phrase holds already, as a word or a minus-word, is not added again. A phrase in quotes
 is left as it is.

 synonyms: a file with the columns word and synonyms, as the step `synonyms` gives; the file is
 read like `read` does.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv add-minus-words купить write out.csv
