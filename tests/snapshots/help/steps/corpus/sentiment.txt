
 Usage: semkit read {IN} sentiment [OPTIONS] [DICTIONARY]

 The score of each phrase by the words of `scores` it holds: their mean, min or max.

 scores: a dictionary word → number, e.g. {"отличный": 5, "плохой": -3}; words match in any case
 and with ё = е, and a word repeated in the phrase counts each time. what: "mean", "min" or "max".
 A phrase with no scored word gets an empty value.

 dictionary: a file of two columns, a word and its score; the first row is the header, the file is
 read like `read` does.

 The step writes the scores to a new column <column>_sentiment.

 Example: semkit read core.csv sentiment купить write out.csv
