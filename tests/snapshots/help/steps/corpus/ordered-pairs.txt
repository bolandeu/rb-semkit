
 Usage: semkit read [IN] ordered-pairs [OPTIONS]

 Every ordered pair of two different items of a list: a table first, second («a», «b» → a b, b a).

 Each item counts once, in the order of its first row; empty cells are no items. n items give n x
 (n - 1) pairs.
 limit: the most pairs the result may hold; a larger result is refused before any is made.

 Example: semkit read --text "диван" --text "кресло" ordered-pairs write -
 -> диван, кресло; кресло, диван

 The step makes a table of its result; it replaces the current table, its first text column is the
 working one.

│                       is made.                                                                   │
