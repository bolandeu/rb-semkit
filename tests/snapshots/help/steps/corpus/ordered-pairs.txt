
 Usage: semkit read {IN} ordered-pairs [OPTIONS]

 Every ordered pair of two different items of a list: a table first, second («a», «b» → a b, b a).

 Each item counts once, in the order of its first row; empty cells are no items.
 limit: the most pairs the result may hold; a larger result raises InvalidArguments before any is
 made.

 The step makes a table of its result; it replaces the current table, its first text column is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv ordered-pairs write out.csv

│ --limit        <int>  the most pairs the result may hold; a larger result raises                 │
│                       InvalidArguments before any is made.                                       │
│                       [default: 1048576]                                                         │
│ --help                Show this message and exit.                                                │
