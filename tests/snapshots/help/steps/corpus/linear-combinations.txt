
 Usage: semkit read [IN] linear-combinations [OPTIONS]

 Every combination of one item from each column of the table, one column per list (the Cartesian
 product).

 The first column changes slowest: купить, заказать × диван, стол → купить диван, купить стол,
 заказать диван… The first column of the result, phrase, holds each combination as one phrase: its
 items joined by one space, in the column order; the columns of the lists follow it. The empty
 cells of a column are skipped, so the lists may differ in length; a column with no items is
 refused, and so is a column named phrase.
 limit: the most rows the result may hold; a larger result is refused before any is made.

 The step makes a table of its result; it replaces the current table, its first text column is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read combine.csv linear-combinations --limit 1000 write out.csv
