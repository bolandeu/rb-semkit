
 Usage: semkit read {IN} split-text [OPTIONS]

 Every cell cut into phrases at sentence ends and clause punctuation; one row per phrase, none for
 an empty cell.

 The punctuation is dropped, whitespace collapsed, case kept. A dash between words (— – -) and the
 point of an abbreviation of the bundled word rules («г.», «ул.», «т. е.», «тел.», «руб.») do not
 cut a phrase and are dropped too: «Склад временного хранения — это место» is one phrase, «г.
 Краснознаменск» is «г Краснознаменск». A hyphen or an apostrophe inside a word and a point or a
 comma inside a number stay («31.03.2026», «2,5»). The rows of the phrases repeat the other columns
 of their source row and keep its index.

 The step cuts each cell of the working column; the table of the phrases replaces the current one,
 each row keeps the other columns of its source row, and the working column stays.

 Example: semkit read core.csv split-text write out.csv
