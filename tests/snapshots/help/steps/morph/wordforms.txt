
 Usage: semkit read [IN] wordforms [OPTIONS] [WORD]

 Every wordform of the lemma of a word, alphabetical, each once: «диваны» → «диван», «дивана», …

 word: the word, in any form. The forms are those of its lemma, in lower case; the word itself is
 not repeated («диван» gives «дивана», «диване», … but not «диван»). A word the dictionary lacks
 gives no forms.

 The step ignores the current table: the table of the wordforms replaces it, its column wordform is
 the working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv wordforms диван write out.csv
