
 Usage: semkit read {IN} wordforms [OPTIONS] [WORD]

 Every wordform of the lemma of `word`, alphabetical, each once: «диваны» → [«диван», «дивана»,
 ...].

 As «Генерация всех словоформ»: the forms of the lemma of the word in lower case; the input word is
 not repeated (ADR 0005).

 The step ignores the current table: the table of the wordforms replaces it, its column wordform is
 the working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv wordforms купить write out.csv
