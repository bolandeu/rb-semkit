
 Usage: semkit read [IN] lemmatize [OPTIONS]

 Each word of a phrase replaced by its lemma, in lower case.

 fallback: for a word missing from the bundled dictionary. Not given: the word goes to pymorphy3 if
 its dictionary knows the word, else to the suffix rule, and often stays as it is («тиктокерами»);
 "pymorphy": such a word takes the best guess of pymorphy3 («тиктокерами» → «тиктокер»).

 Example: semkit read --text "купила диваны" lemmatize write -
 -> купить диван

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.
