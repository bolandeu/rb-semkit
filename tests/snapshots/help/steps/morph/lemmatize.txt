
 Usage: semkit read {IN} lemmatize [OPTIONS]

 Each word of a phrase replaced by its lemma, in lower case: «купила диваны» → «купить диван».

 fallback: None — a word missing from the bundled dictionary goes to pymorphy3 if its dictionary
 knows the word, else to the suffix rule, and often stays as it is («тиктокерами»); "pymorphy" —
 such a word takes the best guess of pymorphy3 («тиктокерами» → «тиктокер»). The fallback is for
 the bundled lemmatizer.

 The step rewrites the working column; with --as NAME it writes the result to a new column NAME.

 Example: semkit read core.csv lemmatize write out.csv
