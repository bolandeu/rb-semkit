
 Usage: semkit read [IN] synonym-candidates [OPTIONS]

 Synonym candidates for the words of the core, from a thesaurus (RuWordNet) and word vectors
 (navec).

 A candidate is a proposal for the Human to pick, not a synonym: the thesaurus gives the words of
 the same synset (score 1), the word vectors the nearest words (the score is the cosine, 4 places).
 The step works on lemmas; stopwords and words with digits are skipped, and so are candidates of
 several words. The report has one row for each candidate: word, candidate, source, score and
 in_core (whether the candidate is a word of the core), words in the order of the core, each with
 the candidates of the thesaurus first, then of the vectors from the nearest.
 top: how many of the nearest words the word vectors give for each word.
 all: give every candidate. By default only the candidates that occur in the core.
 as_synonyms: the report as the file the steps with `--synonyms` read: the columns word and
 synonyms (the candidates of a word joined by a space), one row for each word that has some.

 source: the data to take candidates from, pulled once with `semkit models pull NAME`: ruwordnet
 (the thesaurus), navec (the word vectors) or both.

 The step makes a table of its result; it replaces the current table, its first text column is the
 working one. Its candidates are for the Human to pick; the picked go to --synonyms of the steps
 that take them.

 Example: semkit read core.csv synonym-candidates write out.csv
