
 Usage: semkit read [IN] relate-phrases [OPTIONS]

 The class of each phrase at the other phrases of the table, the phrase it belongs to, its group
 and the reason.

 A phrase is its words as lemmas; a preposition, a conjunction or a particle is dropped, except the
 question words (the bundled list @meaning/question) and the words to keep. A word with digits
 stays as it is. A unit is a word, or a run of words that a link source knows as one; two units are
 linked when a link source links them. The class: same (the same lemmas in any order, at an earlier
 row), variation (every unit pairs one to one with a unit of an earlier row, equal or linked, at
 least one pair linked), refinement (every unit of another phrase, of any row, pairs with a unit of
 this one, and this one has units left over) or other. A phrase takes the best relation: same, then
 variation, then refinement; for a refinement the closest base (the most units), then the earliest
 row. The columns: class; of (the phrase it is same, a variation or a refinement of; empty for
 other); group (the first row among the phrases whose chains of `of` end in the same phrase);
 reason (lemmas; the links of the units; +word for each unit left over; for other the phrases that
 are its refinements, «base of: …», or the units unlinked to the nearest phrase, «unlinked: …»). An
 empty cell is other, with an empty group.
 to: compare every phrase only with this one; of is this phrase or empty.
 keep: more words a phrase never drops, as the question words.

 Example: semkit read --text "разработка сайтов" --text "сайт разработка" --text "разработка сайтов
 под ключ" relate-phrases write -
 -> разработка сайтов, other, , разработка сайтов, base of: сайт разработка | разработка сайтов под
 ключ; сайт разработка, same, разработка сайтов, разработка сайтов, lemmas; разработка сайтов под
 ключ, refinement, разработка сайтов, разработка сайтов, +ключ
 Example: semkit read --text "угловой диван" --text "кресло" relate-phrases --to "диван" write -
 -> угловой диван, refinement, диван, угловой диван, +угловой; кресло, other, , кресло, unlinked:
 кресло | диван

 The step adds the columns class, of, group and reason right after the working column; the rows
 stay. The caller gives the phrases in its order (by frequency): the first phrase of a group is the
 one kept.
