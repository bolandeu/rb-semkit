
 Usage: semkit read {IN} off-topic [OPTIONS]

 The distance of each phrase of the core from the centre of the core, by meaning.

 threshold: the similarity from 0 to 1 a phrase needs to the centre to be on topic.

 The centre is the mean of the phrase vectors of the core, each weighed by its frequency with freq
 and by 1 without it; with seed, the mean of the phrase vectors of the seed phrases. The columns:
 off_topic_score (1 minus the similarity of the phrase to the centre, from 0 to 1, 4 places) and
 off_topic (yes when the similarity is below the threshold, else no); an empty cell gets none. No
 row is dropped.

 model: the encoder, pulled once with `semkit models pull NAME`: e5-small, bge-m3, potion, or
 hf:<id> of a model of the Hugging Face hub. Without --threshold the step takes the threshold of
 the model; a hub model has none.
 freq: the column of the frequencies of the phrases in the current table; the centre of the core
 weighs each phrase by it. Without it, every phrase weighs the same.
 seed: the phrases whose centre is the topic, in place of the core: a list of your own as @FILE or
 @NAME, or comma-separated.

 The step adds the columns off_topic_score and off_topic right after the working column; the rows
 stay.

 Example: semkit read core.csv off-topic --freq freq write out.csv
