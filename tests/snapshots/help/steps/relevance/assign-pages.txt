
 Usage: semkit read {IN} assign-pages [OPTIONS] [PAGES]

 The best two pages of the site for each phrase of the core, by meaning.

 The columns: best_page, best_similarity, second_page and second_similarity (4 places); the second
 page and its similarity are empty when the table has one page; an empty cell gets none. No row is
 dropped.

 pages: a file with the pages of the site; the file is read like `read` does. Its columns: url, and
 text (the text of the page: its title, H1 and body). A text longer than the input of the model is
 cut by the encoder.
 model: the encoder, pulled once with `semkit models pull NAME`: e5-small, bge-m3, potion, or
 hf:<id> of a model of the Hugging Face hub. Without --threshold the step takes the threshold of
 the model; a hub model has none.

 The step adds the columns best_page, best_similarity, second_page and second_similarity right
 after the working column; the rows stay.

 Example: semkit read core.csv assign-pages --pages pages.csv write out.csv
