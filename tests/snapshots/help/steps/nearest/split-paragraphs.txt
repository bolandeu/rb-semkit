
 Usage: semkit read [IN] split-paragraphs [OPTIONS]

 Texts into their paragraphs: a report of one row per paragraph.

 A paragraph is a block between empty lines; the lines of a block are joined by a space. A heading
 line (a short line without a sentence end: . ! ? …, followed by text) is a paragraph of its own.
 Spaces at the ends are trimmed; an empty text gives no rows. The columns: url, number (from 1 in
 each text) and paragraph.

 Example: semkit read --whole article.txt split-paragraphs write -
 -> , 1, Купить диван в Москве. Диван угловой, диван недорогой. Углы дивана и кресла.

 A heading line has up to 12 words. The step takes a text file read with --whole, or a table of url
 and text: its working column as the texts (the first other text column when it is the url column)
 and its url column; the report replaces the current table.
