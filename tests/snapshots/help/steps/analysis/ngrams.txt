
 Usage: semkit read {IN} ngrams [OPTIONS]

 Every run of `n` words over all phrases, counted: a table ngram, count, docs, sum and forms.

 count: occurrences. docs: in how many distinct index values (texts, when `sk.split_text` cut the
 phrases) the gram occurs at least once; the add-in has no such column, it is a semkit addition, so
 the conformance cases compare count and sum only. sum: with `weight`. forms: with `lemmas`, the
 word forms met for the gram, most frequent first, joined by ', ' («диваны, диван»); for a gram of
 several words its distinct spellings, at most 5.

 Sorted by count, biggest first, then by ngram. Words are compared by case and ё.
 lemmas: count lemmas of the words («диваны» is «диван»), by `lemmatizer`.
 stopwords: False makes the runs of words that are no stopwords; a stopword between two of them
 stays inside the gram («купить диван в москве» gives «купить диван» and «диван в москве»), as
 «Биграмм без учета стоп-слов»; stopwords are found in any case (ADR 0005).
 weight: a numeric column of the table; `sum` adds its value once per occurrence.

 The step counts the working column; the table replaces the current one, its column ngram is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv ngrams write out.csv
