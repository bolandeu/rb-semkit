
 Usage: semkit read {IN} counts [OPTIONS] [WHAT]

 One count per phrase, as a Series or a new column `<column>_<what>`.

 what: "words" and "chars" — the words and the characters of the phrase; "count" — how many times
 the same phrase stands in the core; "number" — which occurrence of it this row is (1 for the
 first). Phrases are the same by case and ё (ADR 0005); an empty cell has no count (empty value).
 "share" — the cumulative share of the sum of `weight` (0..1) in row order; "share_of_total" — the
 value over the sum; both are empty when the sum is 0. "abc" — the class by the cumulative share
 from the heaviest phrase down: "A" up to 80 %, "B" up to 95 %, "C" the rest. "share",
 "share_of_total" and "abc" need `weight`.

 The step writes the count of each phrase to a new column <column>_count.

 Example: semkit read core.csv counts купить write out.csv
