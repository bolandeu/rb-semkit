
 Usage: semkit read {IN} bigrams [OPTIONS]

 Pairs of words of a phrase in any order, counted: «купить диван» and «диван купить» both give
 «диван+купить».

 Per phrase the words are sorted and a repeated word counts once. The table and its arguments are
 those of `ngrams`.

 The step counts the working column; the table replaces the current one, its column ngram is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv bigrams write out.csv
