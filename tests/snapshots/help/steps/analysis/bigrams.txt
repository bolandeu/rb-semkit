
 Usage: semkit read {IN} bigrams [OPTIONS]

 Pairs of words of a phrase in any order, counted: «купить диван» and «диван купить» both give
 «диван+купить».

 Per phrase the words are sorted and a repeated word counts once. The table is that of ngrams.
 lemmas: count lemmas of the words («диваны» is «диван»).
 stopwords: on, every word counts; off (--no-stopwords), only the words that are no stopwords make
 pairs.
 weight: a column of numbers of the table (a frequency); sum adds its value once per occurrence.

 The step counts the working column; the table replaces the current one, its column ngram is the
 working one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv bigrams write out.csv
