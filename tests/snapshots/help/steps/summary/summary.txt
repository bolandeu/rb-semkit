
 Usage: semkit read [IN] summary [OPTIONS]

 The count, mean, median, min and max of each numeric column of a table: a row per column.

 The columns: column, the name; count, the cells with a number; mean, median, min, max. Numbers are
 rounded to 4 decimals, as the comparisons round; a column of whole numbers gives whole numbers
 where they are whole. A column of text and a column of true / false are left out. A table without
 a numeric column is refused. parse-html, then text-stats, then summary gives the table «all pages:
 mean, median».

 The step reports on the whole current table, whatever the working column is; its table replaces
 the current one.

 Example: semkit read core.csv summary write out.csv
