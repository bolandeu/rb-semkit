
 Usage: semkit read {IN} compare-stats [OPTIONS] [COMPETITORS]

 The metrics of text-stats, mine against the competitors: a row per metric.

 tolerance: how far from the median a metric is still within, a share of the median (0.3 is ±30 %).

 The columns: metric; mine; median, min, max, the metric over the competitors; delta, mine -
 median; verdict, «below», «within» or «above», mine against median x (1 ± tolerance), and when the
 median is 0, «within» if mine is 0, else «above». Numbers are rounded to 4 decimals; the counts
 (chars, chars_no_spaces, words, unique) stay whole numbers where they are whole. The only norm is
 the median.

 competitors: a file with the texts of the competitors, one per row, in its first text column; the
 file is read like `read` does.

 The step takes the working column as my text (its cells, one per line); the table of the
 comparison replaces the current one.

 Example: semkit read --whole mine.txt compare-stats --competitors competitors.csv write out.csv
