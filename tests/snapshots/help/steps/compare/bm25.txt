
 Usage: semkit read {IN} bm25 [OPTIONS] [QUERY]

 Okapi BM25 of each text for a query: one score per text, rounded to 4 decimals.

 Terms are the lemmas without stopwords, on both sides; a term repeated in the query counts once. A
 word without a lemma (a single letter, an abbreviation or a unit: «г», «руб», «см») is no term.
 The length of a text is its number of terms. idf = ln((N - df + 0.5) / (df + 0.5) + 1); N, df and
 the average length are taken over the given texts, so to rank my text among the competitors give
 all of them together. score = sum over the terms of idf x tf x (k1 + 1) / (tf + k1 x (1 - b + b x
 length / average length)). A text without a term of the query scores 0.
 query: the query, a phrase.
 k1: how fast the score of a term saturates with its count (usually 1.2..2).
 b: how much the length of a text lowers the score, 0..1 (0: not at all).

 The step takes each cell of the working column as a text and writes its score for the query to a
 new column <column>_bm25; to rank my text among the competitors, put all the texts in the working
 column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv bm25 'купить диван' write out.csv
