
 Usage: semkit read {IN} coverage-meaning [OPTIONS] [TEXT]

 For each core phrase, the paragraph nearest it by meaning, and whether the text covers the phrase.

 threshold: the similarity from 0 to 1 from which a paragraph covers a phrase.

 The report has a row for each phrase, in its order and with its index: url and number (of the
 paragraph), the paragraph, similarity (4 places) and covered (yes or no); with clusters also
 cluster_covered. A phrase with no words, or a text with no paragraphs, gives an empty paragraph,
 no similarity and covered no.

 text: a text file (.txt), or a table of url and text, that is cut into paragraphs like
 `split-paragraphs` does; the table file is read like `read` does.
 cluster: the column of the clusters of the phrases in the current table (as `cluster-meaning`
 gives it); every phrase also gets cluster_covered, yes when any phrase of its cluster is covered.
 model: the encoder, pulled once with `semkit models pull NAME`: e5-small, bge-m3, potion, or
 hf:<id> of a model of the Hugging Face hub. Without --threshold the step takes the threshold of
 the model; a hub model has none.

 The step takes the working column as the core and adds the columns url, number, paragraph,
 similarity and covered (and cluster_covered) right after it; the rows stay.

 Example: semkit read core.csv coverage-meaning --text article.txt write out.csv
