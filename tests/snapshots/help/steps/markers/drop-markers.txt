
 Usage: semkit read [IN] drop-markers [OPTIONS] [LISTS]

 Items of marker lists found in a phrase, as whole words, in any case, ё as е; all lists in one
 pass.

 with_prepositions: only the markers right after a toponym preposition («в», «во», «на», «по»,
 «из», «до», «г», «и»; as written, «в,» is none) count, and the preposition goes with them; a
 marker without one stays. A preposition takes the longest item right after it. remove cuts the
 pair, extract gives it («в Москве»), find marks the phrases where such a pair stands.
 sep: what joins the markers extract gives.

 A word glued to punctuation is not matched («москве,»). find, remove and extract take the same
 markers: left to right, for each word the longest item that starts there (in words, then in
 characters; a tie keeps the list order: the lists in the given order, your own lists last), and
 the words of a taken item start no other («Нижний Новгород», not «Нижний Новгород, Новгород»).
 extract gives them as the list spells them («Москве»; «как сделать», not «как»), in word order.
 remove keeps the case of the other words.

 Example: semkit read --text "отели в москве" --text "купить диван" drop-markers --lists geo write
 -
 -> купить диван

 lists: the marker lists, comma-separated: a bundled one by its name (geo) or as @markers/geo, or a
 list of your own as @FILE (one phrase per line, or the first column of a table) or @NAME of a word
 list.

 drop-markers drops the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.
