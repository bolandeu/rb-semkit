
 Usage: semkit read {IN} drop-markers [OPTIONS]

 Items of marker lists found in a phrase, as whole words, in any case, ё as е; all lists in one
 pass.

 lists: names of the bundled lists (geo, mosmetro, districts, MO, citiesRu, questions, fun, DIY,
 edu, game, sale, rent, reviews, color, material, origin, cars, names). user: your own lists,
 `{"name": ["phrase", …]}`. A word glued to punctuation is not matched («москве,»). find, remove
 and extract take the same markers: left to right, for each word the longest item that starts there
 (in words, then in characters; a tie keeps the list order: the lists in the given order, user
 lists last), and the words of a taken item start no other («Нижний Новгород», not «Нижний
 Новгород, Новгород»). extract gives them as the list spells them («Москве»; «как сделать», not
 «как»), in word order, joined by `sep`. remove keeps the case of the other words.
 with_prepositions: only the markers right after a toponym preposition («в», «во», «на», «по»,
 «из», «до», «г», «и»; as written, «в,» is none) count, and the preposition goes with them; a
 marker without one stays. A preposition takes the longest item right after it, as above. remove
 cuts the pair, extract gives it («в Москве»), find is True where such a pair stands.

 drop-markers drops the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv drop-markers write out.csv
