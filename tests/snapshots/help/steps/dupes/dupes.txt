
 Usage: semkit read {IN} dupes [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 Duplicates of the phrases, by kind.

 kind: exact (the same text), explicit (the same in any case, with ё as е and any spaces), implicit
 (the same words in another order) or lemmas (the same lemmas in any order).
 action: mark_except_first (true for each duplicate, false for the first of its group), mark_all
 (true for every member of a group of 2 or more), drop (keep the first, drop the rest).

 The step marks the duplicates of the working column in a new column <column>_dupes; with --action
 drop it keeps only the first row of each group.

 Example: semkit read core.csv dupes lemmas --action mark_except_first write out.csv
