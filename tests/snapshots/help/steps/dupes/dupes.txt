
 Usage: semkit read {IN} dupes [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 Duplicates by `kind`: exact, explicit (case, ё→е, spaces), implicit (other word order) or lemmas.

 `action`: mark_except_first (a bool, the first of a group is False), mark_all (every member of a
 group of 2+), drop (keep the first, drop the rest). With `against` a phrase is a duplicate when
 its key is in that Series.

 The step marks the duplicates of the working column in a new column <column>_dupes; with --action
 drop it keeps only the first row of each group.

 Example: semkit read core.csv dupes exact --action mark_except_first write out.csv
