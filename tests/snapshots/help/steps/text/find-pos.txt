
 Usage: semkit read {IN} find-pos [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 Words of one part of speech: "noun", "adj", "verb" or "adverb" (a participle is none of them).

 A word is looked up as written, with its punctuation («диван,» is not a noun). An adjective is a
 word coded «a», or a word with no code, a pronoun or a particle whose lemma ends like an adjective
 («какой»); a word with a capital is looked up by that rule only («Быстро» is an adjective, as in
 the add-in).
 lemmatizer: what gives the part of speech and the lemma of a word; None is the bundled !SEMTools
 dictionary.

 find-pos marks the rows where the matcher finds something, in a new column <column>_find of true
 and false.

 Example: semkit read core.csv find-pos noun write out.csv
