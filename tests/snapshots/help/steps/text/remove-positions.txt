
 Usage: semkit read {IN} remove-positions [OPTIONS]

 The first `count` characters of a phrase, or the last ones with `last=True` (remove and extract).

 remove gives '' when the phrase has `count` characters or fewer; extract then gives the whole
 phrase.

 remove-positions removes what the matcher finds from the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv remove-positions --count 2 write out.csv
