
 Usage: semkit read [IN] keep-pos [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 Words of one part of speech (a participle is none of them).

 kind: "noun", "adj", "verb" or "adverb".

 A word is looked up as written, with its punctuation («диван,» is not a noun). An adjective is a
 word coded «a», or a word with no code, a pronoun or a particle whose lemma ends like an adjective
 («какой»); a word with a capital is looked up in lower case («Быстро» is an adverb, not an
 adjective).

 keep-pos keeps only the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv keep-pos noun write out.csv
