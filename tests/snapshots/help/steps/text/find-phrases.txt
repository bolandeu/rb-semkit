
 Usage: semkit read {IN} find-phrases [OPTIONS] [ITEMS]

 Phrases of a list, found as whole words («угловой диван» is not in «угловойдиван» or «угловой
 диван,»).

 Words compare in any case, ё as е; punctuation glued to a word counts. find, remove and extract
 take the same phrases, as `sk.markers(user=...)` does: left to right, for each word the longest
 phrase that starts there (in words, then in characters; a tie keeps the list order), and the words
 of a taken phrase start no other. remove keeps the case of the other words; extract gives the
 phrases as the list spells them, joined by ', '.

 find-phrases marks the rows where the matcher finds something, in a new column <column>_find of
 true and false.

 Example: semkit read core.csv find-phrases купить write out.csv
