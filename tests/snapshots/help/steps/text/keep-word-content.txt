
 Usage: semkit read {IN} keep-word-content [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 Words by what they hold; a word is tested as written, with its punctuation («красный,» does not
 end in «ный»).

 kind: "starts", "ends", "contains" (with `pattern`: a word part or a list of them, any one is
 enough; case and ё do not count); "only_latin" (a latin letter, no digit and no Russian letter:
 «IKEA», «Wi-Fi»); "has_latin"; "only_digits" (a number: «2», «1,5», «-5»); "has_digits"; "mixed"
 (a digit and a letter: «iphone15»).

 keep-word-content keeps only the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv keep-word-content starts write out.csv
