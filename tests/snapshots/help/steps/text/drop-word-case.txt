
 Usage: semkit read {IN} drop-word-case [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 Words by the case of their letters (find and extract only); digits and punctuation do not count.

 kind: "lower" (no capital letter: «x5»); "Capital" (each run of letters starts with a capital, the
 rest small: «Москве», not «iPhone» or «Из-за»); "CAPS" (no small letter: «BMW», «В»).

 drop-word-case drops the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv drop-word-case Capital write out.csv
