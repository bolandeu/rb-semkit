
 Usage: semkit read {IN} remove-cells [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 A whole cell is found or not. remove clears a found cell to '' ("empty" drops the rows); extract
 keeps the cell, or '' if not found.

 kind: "contains", "starts", "ends" (with `items`: text parts, case and ё do not count; or with
 `prepositions=True`: the first, the last or any word is a bundled preposition, as written, «для,»
 is none); "regex" (with `pattern`); "empty" (no characters at all: a cell of spaces is not empty);
 by occurrence among the cells, in any case and ё as е: "unique" (once), "repeated" (more than
 once), "first", "last" occurrence. Empty cells are never repeats of each other.

 remove-cells removes what the matcher finds from the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv remove-cells contains write out.csv
