
 Usage: semkit read {IN} extract-around [OPTIONS] [PATTERN]

 The part of the phrase before (`after=False`) or after the first (`last=False`) or last occurrence
 of `pattern`.

 The pattern is text as typed, found anywhere, also inside a word («в» is found in «диван»). remove
 finds the first occurrence in any case and the last one in the case written; extract matches the
 case written. ё = е. `include=True` adds the occurrence itself to the part. A phrase without the
 pattern has no part: remove leaves it, extract gives ''. Remove and extract only.

 extract-around keeps only what the matcher finds in the working column; an empty cell where it
 finds nothing.

 Example: semkit read core.csv extract-around '\d+' write out.csv
