
 Usage: semkit read [IN] remove-phrases [OPTIONS] [ITEMS]

 Phrases of a list, found as whole words («угловой диван» is not in «угловойдиван» or «угловой
 диван,»).

 items: the phrases to find.

 Words compare in any case, ё as е; punctuation glued to a word counts. find, remove and extract
 take the same phrases, as the markers of your own lists: left to right, for each word the longest
 phrase that starts there (in words, then in characters; a tie keeps the list order), and the words
 of a taken phrase start no other. remove keeps the case of the other words; extract gives the
 phrases as the list spells them, joined by ', '.

 Example: semkit read --text "купить угловой диван" remove-phrases "угловой диван" write -
 -> купить

 lemmas: compare the lemmas of the words (the bundled dictionary): «дешевые диваны» is «дешевый
 диван».

 remove-phrases removes what the matcher finds from the working column.
