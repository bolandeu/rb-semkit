
 Usage: semkit read [IN] remove-spaces [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 The spaces to remove (remove only).

 kind: "all"; "left" and "right" (at that end only); "both" (at both ends); "extra" (runs collapsed
 to one space, none at the ends). "left", "right" and "both" keep the spaces inside.

 Example: semkit read --text "  купить   диван " remove-spaces extra write -
 -> купить диван

 remove-spaces removes what the matcher finds from the working column.
