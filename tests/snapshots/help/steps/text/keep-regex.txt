
 Usage: semkit read {IN} keep-regex [OPTIONS] [PATTERN]

 What the regular expression `pattern` matches in a phrase, case-sensitive, searched anywhere.

 find: the pattern matches. remove: every match is cut out. extract: the first match, or with
 `all_matches=True` every match, joined by a space.
 invert: the found part is the text the pattern does NOT match: remove keeps only the matches,
 joined with nothing between («диван 120x200» with \d+ → «120200»); extract gives the text outside
 the matches («диван x»); find is True where such text is left.

 keep-regex keeps only the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv keep-regex купить write out.csv
