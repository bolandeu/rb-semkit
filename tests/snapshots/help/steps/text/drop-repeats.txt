
 Usage: semkit read {IN} drop-repeats [OPTIONS]

 Words that repeat an earlier word of the phrase (find and remove; remove keeps the first one).

 mode: "exact" — the same word (case and ё do not count, punctuation does: «диван, диван» has no
 repeat); "wordforms" — the same lemma.
 scope: "all" words; only "adjacent" repeats («диван диван»); only words with a latin ("en") or a
 cyrillic ("ru") letter («iphone13», «wi-fi-роутер»); only "numbers" («2», «1,5»).
 lemmatizer: for "wordforms"; None is the bundled one.

 drop-repeats drops the rows where the matcher finds something in the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv drop-repeats write out.csv
