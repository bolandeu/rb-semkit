
 Usage: semkit read {IN} extract-regex [OPTIONS] [PATTERN]

 What a regular expression matches in a phrase, case-sensitive, searched anywhere.

 pattern: the regular expression; \w, \b and \d take Cyrillic letters too.
 all_matches: extract gives every match, joined by a space; by default the first.
 invert: the found part is the text the pattern does not match: remove keeps only the matches,
 joined with nothing between («диван 120x200» with \d+ → «120200»); extract gives the text outside
 the matches («диван x»); find marks the phrases where such text is left.

 find marks the phrases where the pattern matches; remove cuts out every match; extract gives the
 first match.

 extract-regex keeps only what the matcher finds in the working column; an empty cell where it
 finds nothing.

 Example: semkit read core.csv extract-regex '\d+' write out.csv
