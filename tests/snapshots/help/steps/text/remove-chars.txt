
 Usage: semkit read [IN] remove-chars [OPTIONS] [KIND]

 The characters of one type.

 kind: "latin" (A-Z a-z), "cyrillic" (А..ӿ and Ё: not the capitals Є І Ї), "digits" (0-9),
 "letters_digits" (letters that have a case, and 0-9), "punctuation" (every other character but
 whitespace: also «-», «№» and letters without a case; a point or a comma between two digits is
 none, so «2.5» and «541,67» stay), "upper" (upper-case letters), "ua" (the Ukrainian letters і ї є
 ґ).

 remove cuts the characters out; a punctuation character leaves a space («Диван-кровать» → «Диван
 кровать»). extract keeps latin, cyrillic and digits with the whitespace between them («120x200» →
 «120200»); for the other kinds it gives the runs joined by a space.

 Example: semkit read --text "диван 120x200" remove-chars digits write -
 -> диван x

 remove-chars removes what the matcher finds from the working column.
