
 Usage: semkit read {IN} extract-positions [OPTIONS]

 The first `count` characters of a phrase, or the last ones with `last=True` (remove and extract).

 remove gives '' when the phrase has `count` characters or fewer; extract then gives the whole
 phrase.

 extract-positions keeps only what the matcher finds in the working column; an empty cell where it
 finds nothing.

 Example: semkit read core.csv extract-positions --count 2 write out.csv
