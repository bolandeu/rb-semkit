
 Usage: semkit read [IN] find-word-position [OPTIONS]

 Words by their place in the phrase; exactly one way to pick them is given, N counts from 1.

 first / last: the first or last N words.
 nth: the N-th word.
 to_middle / at_most: the leading words while they hold half the characters of the phrase at most,
 or N characters at most, and always the first word («купить диван недорого», 12 characters at most
 → «купить диван»).

 remove gives the other words.

 Example: semkit read --text "купить диван" --text "кресло" find-word-position --nth 2 write -
 -> купить диван, true; кресло, false

 find-word-position marks the rows where the matcher finds something, in a new column <column>_find
 of true and false.
