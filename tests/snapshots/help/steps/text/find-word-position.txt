
 Usage: semkit read {IN} find-word-position [OPTIONS]

 Words by their place in the phrase; give exactly one argument, N counts from 1.

 first / last: the first or last N words. nth: the N-th word. to_middle / at_most: the leading
 words while they hold half the characters of the phrase at most, or N characters at most, and
 always the first word («купить диван недорого» with at_most=12 → «купить диван»). remove gives the
 other words.

 find-word-position marks the rows where the matcher finds something, in a new column <column>_find
 of true and false.

 Example: semkit read core.csv find-word-position write out.csv
