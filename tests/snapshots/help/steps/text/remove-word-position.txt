
 Usage: semkit read {IN} remove-word-position [OPTIONS]

 Words by their place in the phrase; give exactly one argument, N counts from 1.

 first / last: the first or last N words.
 nth: the N-th word.
 to_middle / at_most: the leading words while they hold half the characters of the phrase at most,
 or N characters at most, and always the first word («купить диван недорого» with at_most=12 →
 «купить диван»). remove gives the other words.

 remove-word-position removes what the matcher finds from the working column.

 Example: semkit read core.csv remove-word-position --first 1 write out.csv
