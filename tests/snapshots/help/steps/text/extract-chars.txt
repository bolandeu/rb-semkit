
 Usage: semkit read {IN} extract-chars [OPTIONS]

 The characters of one type.

 kind: "latin" (A-Z a-z), "cyrillic" (А..ӿ and Ё: not the capitals Є І Ї), "digits" (0-9),
 "letters_digits" (letters that have a case, and 0-9), "punctuation" (every other character but
 whitespace: also «-», «№» and letters without a case), "upper" (upper-case letters), "ua" (the
 Ukrainian letters і ї є ґ). remove cuts the characters out; a punctuation character leaves a space
 («Диван-кровать» → «Диван кровать»). extract keeps latin, cyrillic and digits with the whitespace
 between them («120x200» → «120200»); for the other kinds it gives the runs joined by a space.

 extract-chars keeps only what the matcher finds in the working column; an empty cell where it
 finds nothing.

 Example: semkit read core.csv extract-chars --kind latin write out.csv
