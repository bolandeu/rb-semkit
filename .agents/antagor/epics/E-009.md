## Problem

The marketer's query collector glues the phrases of a topic with ad-hoc rules and fails: it drops «как» as a conjunction («как сделать сайт» = «сделать сайт»), does not link parts of speech («создание сайтов» / «создать сайт») or the Latin spelling («web» / «веб»). `dupes --kind meaning` does not fit: it did not glue «создание сайта» with «создание сайтов», and the vector thresholds do not separate (junk 0.91–0.93 among real 0.90–0.95). The rules must live in semkit; the caller keeps only the SERP check.

## Goal

One step, relate-phrases, gives each phrase of a topic its class (same, variation, refinement or other), the phrase it belongs to (of), the first phrase of its group (group) and the reason (the rule and the word link), by dictionaries and lemmas only: no network, no vectors, no LLM at run time. About 1000 phrases run in seconds. The caller sorts the phrases (by frequency) and checks the variations and the other phrases by SERP.

- Given «разработка веб сайтов» and then «разработка web сайтов», and «ремонт стиральных машин бош» and then «ремонт стиральных машин bosch», and «как создать сайт на wordpress» and then «как создать сайт на вордпресс», When the step runs, Then each is a variation (a Latin word is read as Cyrillic, ш and щ are one sound: bosch = бош), and «ремонт стиральных машин bosch» at «ремонт стиральных машин» alone is a refinement [check: tests/relate/test_latin.py]

## Implementation decisions

- A new area of the library, on a DataFrame: the phrases of one table in, the same phrases with the columns class, of, group and reason out; a public function like the other areas, and a step relate-phrases over it (options: --column, --to, --synonyms, --keep).
- Units: a phrase is cut into units, a unit being a word or a run of adjacent words that the thesaurus knows as one sense; all the cuts are tried and the pairing that explains the most words wins. The cut and the lemmas reuse the units of synonym-candidates and the bundled lemmatizer.
- Links between two units come from sources behind one interface: equal lemmas; the thesaurus (one synset, or a POS-synonymy pair of synsets: the thesaurus reader also reads that relation of the same SQLite file); the Latin reading (a Latin word read as Cyrillic by the translit table, ш = щ); the caller's groups of synonyms. No chain of links (a link of a link is no link). Each link gives a reason text.
- Classes: same (the same lemmas in any order), variation (all units pair one to one, equal or linked, one linked at least), refinement (all units of the other phrase are explained and units are left over), other. Same and variation look at earlier rows only; refinement at all rows, by words, not by order. A phrase takes the best relation over the table (same, variation, refinement; for a refinement the closest base, then the earliest row). Every relation points to a phrase that is before it by (the number of units, the row), so the chains of of end; group is the first row among the phrases whose chains end in the same phrase.
- Question words stay in the phrase: a bundled word list (about 20 words) that --keep extends; the other stopwords are dropped. The new word list is built like the others, in the build parts.
- Candidates are found through an index over the units and their linked units, not over all pairs.

## Test seams

- The semkit command line pipeline: semkit read TABLE.jsonl relate-phrases … write -, on small JSONL tables with a small test thesaurus; the real thesaurus only in the checks marked as needing it.

## Out of scope

- SERP, the roles (main, synonym, direction), frequencies, the choice of the main phrase (the caller sorts), vector thresholds, LLM calls
- A bundled list of links: the links of a topic come per run in --synonyms
- A new dictionary or model, a new download: RuWordNet is already a semkit model; DerivBase.Ru, DeriNet.RU and the derivation table alone are not used
- Translation of words (site = сайт), links through two steps, a fifth class
- A change of dupes, ad-groups, synonym-candidates or their --synonyms files
