## Problem

The Human analyses pages (first of all the pages of the own site) given as HTML, but semkit takes only plain text: the text and link zones, title, description and the headings are prepared by hand; there is no mean or median over a list of pages, a page is compared with a list only by the median, the recommended number of occurrences of a word for a text size is counted by hand, a phrase with inserted words («купить недорогие пластиковые окна») is not found, and numbers or the Human's own words cannot be left out of the measures.

## Goal

semkit read pages.jsonl parse-html gives each page its zones (text, links, all, main), title, description and h1 as columns that every text step takes; headings gives the H1-H6 structure; summary gives the mean, median, min and max of any table; compare-stats and compare-grams compare a page with the mean or the median of a list and give the recommended occurrences for a text size; coverage finds phrases with inserted words; numbers and a word list can be left out of the measures.

## Acceptance (Given–When–Then)

- Given the HTML of a page in a column, When parse-html runs, Then the row gets text (visible text without link text), links, all, main (the main content without menus, header and footer), title, description and h1; scripts, styles and hidden elements are never counted
- Given a page with <noindex> text and alt and title attributes, When parse-html runs, Then they count by default; --drop-noindex and --drop-attributes leave them out
- Given a cell that is not HTML or is empty, When parse-html runs, Then its columns are empty and the step summary counts such rows; the run does not stop
- Given pages, When headings runs, Then the report has one row per heading in page order: url, order, level H1-H6, text
- Given any table, When summary runs, Then every numeric column gets a row: count, mean, median, min, max; a table without numeric columns is refused with exit 2 naming the reason
- Given my page and a list of pages, When compare-stats or compare-grams runs with --norm mean, Then the verdict is against the mean; the mean column stands beside the median; --norm median (the default) gives the same output as before plus the mean column
- Given compare-grams with --words N (or my page size by default), When it runs, Then each gram gets need (the norm density x N, rounded), have and add (need minus have, negative to remove), and the median number of occurrences; --per-text adds a density column per page
- Given coverage --gap 2, When it runs on «купить недорогие пластиковые окна» for the phrase «купить пластиковые окна», Then it counts; --gap 0 (the default) gives the output of before; --gap -1 is refused with exit 2
- Given --no-digits or --exclude LIST, When text-stats, compare-stats or compare-grams runs, Then words with a digit or the listed words are left out of the counts as if absent from the text
- Given the test page of the Human (ost-term import) parsed, When the Architect checks before release, Then main holds the sections of the article without the menu and footer and headings lists H1 «Таможенное оформление импорта» and its H2-H3 in order

## Implementation decisions

- HTML parsing module under a thin wrapper: selectolax (lexbor) for the zones, meta tags, headings and noindex; trafilatura with favor_recall for main (measured: docs/plans/html-and-brief.md)
- Step parse-html: a change step on the working column of HTML, adding the zone columns named to match the --pages table of the vector steps (url, text, title, h1); step headings: a report
- Step summary: a report over any table, one row per numeric column
- compare-stats and compare-grams: a mean column, --norm mean|median for the verdict; compare-grams: need, have, add for --words, median occurrences, --per-text density columns
- coverage: --gap N, words inserted between the words of a phrase in exact and lemmas modes
- text-stats, compare-stats, compare-grams: --no-digits and --exclude LIST, applied to the words before counting
